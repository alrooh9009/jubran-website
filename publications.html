May 2024 · Research Paper

A Comparative Study of Data Protection Laws in Saudi Arabia and the United States

This paper compares Saudi Arabia’s integrated Personal Data Protection Law framework with the United States’ sectoral privacy model. It examines individual rights, consent, government access, cross-border transfers, enforcement and the influence of the GDPR, highlighting the different trade-offs each system makes between regulatory coherence, flexibility, privacy and security.

November 2025 · Research Paper

Is Alice Corp. v. CLS Bank Good for Innovation in the Software Industry? Evaluating USPTO and Congressional Responses

This paper evaluates the effect of Alice Corp. v. CLS Bank on software patent eligibility under 35 U.S.C. § 101. It considers the decision’s impact on patent quality, litigation, investment and software innovation, then examines USPTO guidance, congressional reform proposals and the challenges posed by emerging technologies such as artificial intelligence, blockchain and quantum computing.

March 2025 · Research Paper

The Impact of Digital Rights Management (DRM) on Consumer Rights: A Call for Reform

This policy paper argues that common DRM implementations can tilt the balance too far toward copyright holders at the expense of consumer autonomy, ownership expectations, libraries and access. Drawing on cases including Vernor v. Autodesk and Capitol Records v. ReDigi, it proposes stronger minimum consumer rights, clearer transparency rules, a digital first-sale framework and support for less restrictive models.

October 2024 · Research Paper

Loot Boxes and Microtransactions in Video Games: A Comparative Legal Analysis

This comparative paper examines how legal systems classify and regulate loot boxes and microtransactions in video games. It compares gambling-law, transparency, consumer-protection and self-regulatory approaches across the United States, Europe and Asia, with particular attention to children and vulnerable consumers, probability disclosures and the difficulty of fitting digital rewards into traditional gambling concepts.
