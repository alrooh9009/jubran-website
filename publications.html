Performance Shortfalls in Saudi EPC Projects: Testing, Output Guarantees, and Remedies under the Civil Transactions Law

This paper examines how Saudi law should treat EPC assets that are physically complete but fail to achieve contractual output or performance. It argues that Article 168 of the Civil Transactions Law provides a strong statutory basis for obligations to achieve specified results, while Article 179 and related remedial rules require a disciplined distinction between minimum performance floors and economic performance guarantees.

The Impact of Digital Rights Management (DRM) on Consumer Rights: A Call for Reform
