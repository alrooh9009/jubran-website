Profile

Transactions and commercial work

Jubran Alsughayyir is a Saudi legal professional whose current work includes corporate transactions, legal due diligence, financing and investment matters, commercial contracts, governance and regulatory analysis. He supports matters involving Saudi and international counterparties, investment structures, SPVs, funds, sukuk-related documentation and corporate approvals.

Projects and disputes

His experience also includes major construction and EPC matters, commercial claims, litigation support and arbitration preparation. The work has involved delay, access constraints, extension of time, prolongation, owner approvals, contractual effectiveness, entitlement analysis and technical evidence in regulatory and customs matters.

Research as part of practice

This site is the public home of selected research and legal commentary. The writing focuses on questions that matter to practitioners: enforceability, transaction design, structured finance, AI infrastructure, legal due diligence, EPC performance and technical proof in customs classification. The objective is not to restate new rules, but to examine what they change in practice.

Education

Jubran holds two LL.M. degrees from the University of Washington School of Law: Global Business Law (2024) and Intellectual Property Law & Policy (2025), following an LL.B. from Jazan University (2021). He completed the Saudi Ministry of Justice Lawyers Development Program in 2026.
